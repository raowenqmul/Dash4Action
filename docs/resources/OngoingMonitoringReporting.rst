Ongoing Monitoring and Reporting
=====

They say that “what gets measured gets improved”. To this end it is
important to inform users about their carbon footprint in a way that
does not blame them but empowers them to optimise their DRI use to
optimise for minimum carbon emissions.

Reporting both to users and to system operators, as well as funding
bodies and stakeholders is important.

 
* User emails:

   * Regular reporting of power usage and CO2 equivalency to
      individual users and to project PIs

      * Including examples of what this CO2 is equivalent to

   * Reporting of inefficient usage

      * E.g. using a single core of a many-core server

   * Ability for users to query their energy usage
