NetDRIVE Dash4Action: Facility Action Plans for Sustainable Computing 
=====================================================================

**Dash4Action** is funded by `NetDRIVE <https://uknetdrive.org/>`_.

If you are comming to `CIUK 2026 <https://www.sc.stfc.ac.uk/ciuk-2026/>`_ then please join
our breakout session / Workshop on Day Zero. Details of the 2 hour session can be found below.

The workshop will introduce:

* NetDRIVE
* Green DISC
* Dash4Action and its case studies of effective actions taken to improve sustanability at 3 facilities.

The workshop will include drafting sessions using Dash4Action templates and resources so you
will leave with the beginnings of a sustainability action plan for your facility.
